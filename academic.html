Texas Math and Science Coaches Association

TMSCA is a set of competitions throughout the year where schools around Texas, both public and private, compete in 4 sections:

  • Number Sense requires crunching 80 arithmetic problems in 10 minutes using only their mind with no external help
  • Calculator involves in individual using a calculator to solve 80 complex problems in 30 minutes. Problems oculd include complex fractions, probability, and complex geometrics.
  • General math involves solving 50 math problems in 40 minutes, with problems of many different arrays, often requiring up to Village's Algebra 2 class to have understanding of all problems.
  • Lastly, science involves answering 50 multiple-choice science questions in 40 minutes, with topics covering many sciences such as biology, chemistry, and physics.

These competitions increase in difficulty and eventually culminate to the state competition and tests, where it really matters.

Achievements

My main achievements involved getting a perfect score in the state competition on the calculator section for the 2 consecutive years that I took this competition in middle school.

TMSCA taught me many test-taking skills, especially when being under pressure.
